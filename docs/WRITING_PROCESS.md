# Novel Writing Process Breakdown

A comprehensive breakdown of the novel writing journey from initial spark to published manuscript, with specific subtasks the extension can support at each stage.

## Phase 1: Conception & Ideation

### 1.1 Capturing Initial Ideas
**What happens**: The spark - a character, scene, premise, "what if" question, or emotional truth

**Subtasks**:
- Quick capture of fleeting ideas (voice notes, quick text)
- Expand idea prompts ("Tell me more about...")
- Connect related ideas/fragments
- Visual inspiration board (images, mood references)
- Dream logging and interpretation

**Extension Support**:
- `/idea-capture` - quick idea entry with timestamp
- Idea repository with tagging and search
- Automatic prompt: "What makes this compelling to you?"
- Link ideas to potential projects

### 1.2 Exploring the Premise
**What happens**: Testing if there's a novel here, not just a scene or concept

**Subtasks**:
- Identify the central conflict
- Find the story question ("Will X succeed in Y?")
- Determine what's at stake
- Explore thematic possibilities
- Check for originality and fresh angles

**Extension Support**:
- Premise development worksheet
- Stake-raising prompts
- Similar story finder (to understand your unique angle)
- Thematic exploration questions

### 1.3 Initial Character Sketches
**What happens**: Meeting the people who will live in this story

**Subtasks**:
- Name and basic appearance
- Core desire/motivation
- Greatest fear/obstacle
- Distinctive voice/mannerism
- Backstory hints

**Extension Support**:
- Character profile templates (lightweight at this stage)
- Name generators with cultural context
- Voice sample recording (write dialogue snippets)
- Character relationship mapping

## Phase 2: Development & Planning

### 2.1 World Building (if applicable)
**What happens**: Creating the stage where the story unfolds

**Subtasks**:
- Geography and setting details
- Time period and historical context
- Social/political/economic systems
- Magic/technology rules (for genre fiction)
- Cultural norms and conflicts
- Sensory details (how it looks, smells, sounds)

**Extension Support**:
- World-building wiki system
- Rule consistency checker
- Map integration
- Timeline creator for historical/alternate worlds
- Research link repository

### 2.2 Character Development
**What happens**: Deepening understanding of who these people are

**Subtasks**:
- Detailed backstories
- Character arcs (beginning → end state)
- Internal and external conflicts
- Relationships and dynamics
- Character interviews/questionnaires
- Identifying flaws and strengths

**Extension Support**:
- Expanded character profiles
- Character arc visualizer
- Relationship web diagrams
- Character interview prompts
- Flaw/strength balance checker

### 2.3 Plot Architecture
**What happens**: Determining the sequence of events

**Subtasks for Plotters**:
- Three-act structure outline (or alternative structure)
- Scene-by-scene breakdown
- Plot point identification (inciting incident, midpoint, climax)
- Subplot weaving
- Cause-and-effect chain verification
- Chapter summaries

**Subtasks for Pantsers**:
- Directional outline (general trajectory)
- Key scenes/moments to hit
- Character trajectory notes
- Thematic waypoints

**Extension Support**:
- Multiple outlining templates (3-act, Hero's Journey, Save the Cat, Freytag's Pyramid)
- Flexible outline that can evolve
- Scene card system (digital index cards)
- Plot thread color coding
- Timeline visualization
- "Pants-friendly" mode: minimal structure, maximum discovery

### 2.4 Research
**What happens**: Gathering knowledge needed for authenticity

**Subtasks**:
- Historical/cultural research
- Technical/professional expertise
- Location research
- Terminology and jargon
- Interview subject matter experts
- Experience activities firsthand when possible

**Extension Support**:
- Research repository with citations
- Inline fact-checking markers
- Research-to-scene linking (track where you used what)
- Expert contact management
- Reading list tracker

### 2.5 Opening Development
**What happens**: Crafting the crucial first pages/chapter

**Subtasks**:
- Workshop multiple opening lines
- Establish voice and tone
- Introduce protagonist compellingly
- Create immediate questions/hooks
- Set the story world quickly
- First chapter draft and revision

**Extension Support**:
- Opening line workshop (generate variations)
- Hook strength analysis
- First page critique checklist
- Voice consistency checker
- Multiple first chapter versions side-by-side

## Phase 3: First Draft

### 3.1 Session Preparation
**What happens**: Getting into the writing headspace

**Subtasks**:
- Review previous session's work
- Check "what happens next" notes
- Set session goals (word count or scene completion)
- Eliminate distractions
- Perform writing ritual

**Extension Support**:
- Session start checklist
- Previous session summary display
- Daily word count goal setting
- Focus timer (Pomodoro-style)
- Ritual reminder (customizable)

### 3.2 Active Drafting
**What happens**: Getting words on the page

**Subtasks**:
- Write forward (resist editing urge)
- Maintain momentum
- Use placeholders for unknowns ([TK] markers)
- Follow "headlights" where story leads
- Track emotional beats
- Record session-end notes

**Extension Support**:
- Distraction-free writing mode
- [TK] marker system for "to come" items
- Real-time word count
- Session notes capture
- "Don't look back" mode (hide previous text)
- Emotional beat tracker per scene

### 3.3 Chapter Completion
**What happens**: Finishing a chapter unit

**Subtasks**:
- Read-through for major issues
- Check scene/chapter achieves its purpose
- Verify character consistency
- Update timeline
- Mark areas needing attention in revision
- Celebrate progress

**Extension Support**:
- Chapter checklist (purpose, conflict, change)
- Consistency quick-check
- Revision marker placement
- Progress visualization
- Milestone celebrations

### 3.4 Maintaining Continuity
**What happens**: Keeping track of details across growing manuscript

**Subtasks**:
- Character detail consistency (eye color, age, background)
- Timeline accuracy
- Geography/location consistency
- Plot thread tracking
- Foreshadowing notes
- Callback opportunities

**Extension Support**:
- Auto-detection of character details
- Timeline builder from manuscript
- Inconsistency alerts
- Plot thread status board
- Foreshadowing tracker
- Search: "What have I said about X?"

### 3.5 Managing Writer's Block
**What happens**: Working through stuck points

**Subtasks**:
- Identify the problem (don't know what happens / scene is boring / character won't cooperate)
- Try "one true sentence" approach
- Skip ahead to next known scene
- Character interview about the stuck moment
- Change POV or tense experimentally
- Take purposeful break with reflection

**Extension Support**:
- Block diagnosis questions
- "One true sentence" prompt
- Scene skip markers (come back later)
- POV/tense experimental toggle
- Writing prompts specific to current story
- Break timer with reflection prompt

## Phase 4: Developmental Revision

### 4.1 Manuscript Assessment
**What happens**: First big-picture look at completed draft

**Subtasks**:
- Read-through without editing (as a reader)
- Identify structural problems
- Check pacing (too slow/too fast sections)
- Verify character arcs complete
- Assess plot holes and logic gaps
- Theme clarity check

**Extension Support**:
- Reading mode (different formatting/display)
- Big-picture issue tracker
- Pacing visualization
- Character arc completion checklist
- Plot hole identifier
- Thematic thread highlighter

### 4.2 Structural Editing
**What happens**: Fixing major story architecture issues

**Subtasks**:
- Reorder scenes/chapters
- Cut unnecessary scenes
- Add missing scenes
- Strengthen cause-and-effect chains
- Balance subplot prominence
- Adjust timeline

**Extension Support**:
- Scene reordering interface (drag and drop)
- Scene impact analysis (what's lost if cut?)
- Gap identification
- Cause-effect chain visualizer
- Subplot balance meter
- Timeline adjuster with cascade effects

### 4.3 Character Revision
**What happens**: Ensuring characters are consistent and compelling

**Subtasks**:
- Strengthen character voices
- Verify arc consistency
- Deepen motivations
- Add character-revealing details
- Check relationship dynamics
- Remove contradictions

**Extension Support**:
- Voice consistency report per character
- Arc visualization (does character actually change?)
- Motivation check against actions
- Character moment density (enough screen time?)
- Dialogue-only view per character
- Contradiction detector

### 4.4 Pacing Adjustment
**What happens**: Fixing tempo issues

**Subtasks**:
- Tighten slow sections
- Expand rushed moments
- Balance action/reflection
- Add breathing room after intense scenes
- Build tension toward climax
- Check chapter endings (hooks)

**Extension Support**:
- Pacing heat map
- Scene length analyzer
- Tension curve with recommendations
- Action/reflection ratio per chapter
- Chapter ending strength checker
- Conflict density visualization

## Phase 5: Line Editing

### 5.1 Prose Polishing
**What happens**: Making every sentence stronger

**Subtasks**:
- Eliminate weak verbs
- Cut adverbs (especially -ly)
- Replace "very" and other intensifiers
- Vary sentence structure
- Remove redundancies
- Strengthen metaphors and descriptions

**Extension Support**:
- Weak verb highlighter
- Adverb detector with alternatives
- Intensifier finder
- Sentence length variety checker
- Redundancy detector
- Metaphor/simile tracker (avoid repetition)

### 5.2 Dialogue Enhancement
**What happens**: Making conversations crackle

**Subtasks**:
- Remove unnecessary dialogue tags
- Eliminate "on the nose" dialogue
- Add subtext
- Ensure each character sounds distinct
- Read aloud for naturalness
- Trim small talk

**Extension Support**:
- Dialogue tag analyzer
- "As you know, Bob" detector (exposition in dialogue)
- Character voice comparison
- Dialogue-only reader mode
- Text-to-speech for dialogue testing
- Subtext opportunity highlighter

### 5.3 Description Refinement
**What happens**: Making settings and details vivid

**Subtasks**:
- Add sensory details (beyond visual)
- Filter description through character POV
- Cut purple prose
- Balance show/tell
- Strengthen setting as mood
- Remove cliché descriptions

**Extension Support**:
- Sensory detail balance checker (all five senses)
- POV filter consistency
- Purple prose detector
- Show/tell ratio analyzer
- Cliché finder
- Description density heatmap

### 5.4 Reading Aloud Review
**What happens**: Hearing the prose to catch issues

**Subtasks**:
- Listen for rhythm and flow
- Catch awkward phrasings
- Identify tongue-twisters
- Find unintentional rhymes/repetitions
- Test dialogue naturalness
- Note sections you want to skip (weak writing)

**Extension Support**:
- High-quality text-to-speech
- Playback speed control
- Mark-while-listening (voice control)
- Repetition detector (same word/phrase nearby)
- Rhythm analysis (sentence length patterns)
- "Skip tendency" tracker

## Phase 6: Copy Editing

### 6.1 Grammar and Mechanics
**What happens**: Fixing technical errors

**Subtasks**:
- Spell check
- Grammar correction
- Punctuation review
- Capitalization consistency
- Verb tense consistency
- POV consistency (first/third, past/present)

**Extension Support**:
- Advanced grammar checking
- Style guide application (Chicago, AP, etc.)
- Tense consistency checker
- POV slip detector
- Character name consistency (nicknames, titles)
- Formatting consistency

### 6.2 Word Economy
**What happens**: Cutting every unnecessary word

**Subtasks**:
- Eliminate filter words (saw, heard, felt)
- Cut throat-clearing phrases
- Remove doubled words
- Tighten wordy constructions
- Delete redundant descriptors
- Track word count reduction

**Extension Support**:
- Filter word highlighter
- Wordy phrase suggestions
- Redundancy detector
- Before/after word count comparison
- "Could this be shorter?" prompts
- Economy score per chapter

### 6.3 Fact Checking
**What happens**: Verifying accuracy

**Subtasks**:
- Check historical facts
- Verify geography/locations
- Confirm technical details
- Validate timeline math (ages, dates)
- Check quoted material
- Verify cultural accuracy

**Extension Support**:
- Fact marker system (claims to verify)
- Research link integration
- Timeline math validator
- Cultural sensitivity checker
- Citation tracker
- Expert verification log

## Phase 7: Beta Reading & Feedback

### 7.1 Beta Reader Preparation
**What happens**: Getting manuscript ready for feedback

**Subtasks**:
- Create clean reading copy
- Write beta reader questionnaire
- Select appropriate beta readers
- Set feedback timeline
- Provide context (genre, target audience, specific concerns)

**Extension Support**:
- Export clean manuscript (formatted)
- Beta reader question templates
- Feedback request generator
- Beta reader management (who, when, status)
- Specific concern highlighter

### 7.2 Receiving Feedback
**What happens**: Collecting reader responses

**Subtasks**:
- Read all feedback without reacting
- Categorize feedback (plot, character, pacing, confusion, etc.)
- Identify patterns (multiple people note same issue)
- Separate subjective preference from objective problems
- Prioritize changes

**Extension Support**:
- Feedback aggregator (collect all in one place)
- Auto-categorization of comments
- Pattern detection (recurring issues)
- Feedback prioritization matrix
- Version control (pre-beta draft preservation)
- Comment threading (discussions with beta readers)

### 7.3 Implementing Revisions
**What happens**: Acting on feedback

**Subtasks**:
- Address critical issues first
- Make changes aligned with your vision
- Re-check ripple effects
- Know when to reject feedback
- Document what you changed and why

**Extension Support**:
- Feedback-to-revision tracker
- Change log (what was altered)
- Ripple effect checker
- Version comparison
- Rejected feedback notes (with reasoning)
- Second beta reader round management

## Phase 8: Polishing & Finalization

### 8.1 Final Proofread
**What happens**: Last pass for typos and errors

**Subtasks**:
- Fresh eyes review (after time away)
- Read backwards (sentence by sentence)
- Check formatting consistency
- Verify chapter breaks
- Confirm front/back matter
- Professional proofreader (if budget allows)

**Extension Support**:
- "Fresh start" mode (hide previous edits)
- Backward reading interface
- Formatting validator
- Front/back matter templates
- Proofreader communication tools
- Final error tracker

### 8.2 Title & Chapter Titles
**What happens**: Finalizing naming elements

**Subtasks**:
- Workshop book titles
- Test title market appeal
- Create chapter titles (if using)
- Verify title isn't taken/trademarked
- Test title with target readers

**Extension Support**:
- Title brainstorming tools
- Title availability checker
- Chapter title consistency (style/length)
- A/B testing with sample readers
- Title alternative tracker

### 8.3 Synopsis & Query Materials
**What happens**: Creating selling documents

**Subtasks**:
- Write 1-page synopsis
- Write 1-paragraph synopsis
- Write elevator pitch (25 words)
- Create query letter (if seeking agent)
- Identify comparison titles
- Write author bio

**Extension Support**:
- Synopsis templates (length variations)
- Query letter builder
- Comp title suggester
- Pitch tester (clarity, intrigue)
- Agent/publisher research integration
- Marketing materials version control
