# CHAPTER 2: THROUGH THE GLASS
## Scene Beats

Written in the style of Shirley Jackson, emphasizing psychological horror through mounting dread and unreliable perception.

---

## Scene 1: The Waiting (400 words)

**Setting:** Hidden passage behind salon walls, early evening

- Émilie crouched in narrow space, watching through peephole
- Details of cramped passage: rough stone walls, musty air, cobwebs
- Sound of salon activities filtering through walls
- Mother's voice directing specific nobles to certain tables
- Growing tension as Émilie notices patterns in guest placement
- First hints of wrongness: cold spots near certain mirrors
- Strange shadows gathering in corners visible through peephole

---

## Scene 2: The Gathering (500 words)

**Setting:** Main salon viewed through peephole, midnight

- Last regular guests departing
- Twelve specific nobles remaining behind
- Mother locking doors, checking windows
- Servants dismissed except for one elderly butler
- Nobles changing from evening wear into dark robes
- Charles helping move gaming tables aside
- Floor patterns revealed beneath carpets
- Growing supernatural tension as mirrors begin to darken

---

## Scene 3: The Ritual Begins (600 words)

**Setting:** Hidden chamber revealed behind shifting mirrors

- Nobles arranging themselves in precise formation
- Ancient books and silver bowls brought forth
- First blood drawn from each noble's palm
- Chanting in archaic French
- Shadows in mirrors becoming deeper, more alive
- Émilie's growing horror as she realizes truth
- Entity's presence felt through temperature drop
- Mirror surfaces beginning to ripple like water

---

## Scene 4: The Breach (500 words)

**Setting:** Between hidden passage and ritual chamber

- Something noticing Émilie's presence
- Shadow creature testing boundaries in mirrors
- Nobles struggling to maintain containment
- Mother's expert direction of ritual
- Charles showing unexpected magical strength
- Entity reaching toward Émilie's hiding spot
- Émilie's mixed blood causing unexpected reactions
- Émilie cuts hand during chaos

---

## Scene 5: Discovery (500 words)

**Setting:** Hidden passage and ritual chamber

- Entity directly acknowledging Émilie
- Nobles reacting to breach in protective circle
- **Charles slays monster with pistol** (see detailed text)
- Émilie's instinctive magical response
- **Chapter climax:** Entity retreating but marking Émilie
- **Final beat:** Lafayette and Mother's knowing look toward hiding spot
- Lafayette tells mother Émilie needs to learn

## Detailed Prose: Scene 5 Combat Sequence

### At the beginning:

> Charles moved then, drawing something silver from his pocket—not a dagger but a small pistol, its barrel decorated with strange symbols. As the shadow-thing pushed through the widening crack, he fired. The sound was oddly muffled, but the flash illuminated the creature fully for just a moment.
> 
> Émilie stifled a scream. What she saw defied description—a thing of angles that shouldn't exist, of movements that hurt the eye to follow.

### Then a paragraph or two later:

> Where Charles's shot had struck, the shadow-thing collapsed inward, the wound spreading like ripples in dark water. It recoiled toward the mirror's surface, its substance drawn into a void of its own making. The sound it made defied nature—not quite a scream, not quite the shattering of glass, but something that existed in the space between the two.

### And finally, after the ritual is complete and the mirror portals are sealed:

> Trapped between mirror and void, the creature's form began to unravel. It twisted in ways that made Émilie's vision blur, its final howl resonating in the mirrors throughout the salon, before collapsing into nothingness like smoke in a sudden wind.

## Tone & Style

Emphasizes psychological horror through Émilie's shifting understanding of her world, while establishing concrete supernatural elements and historical context.
