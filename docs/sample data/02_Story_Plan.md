# STORY PLAN: 4-ACT HORROR ESCALATION STRUCTURE

## Structure Overview

Selected for its ability to map the progression from mundane salon life through supernatural discovery, revolutionary chaos, and final transformation. Allows balanced development of both supernatural and political horror elements.

---

## Primary Arcs (50,000 words)

### Act 1: Discovery (12,500 words)

- Émilie discovers salon's true purpose
- Initial glimpses of bound entities
- Introduction to class-based magic systems
- Mother's careful protection and guidance
- Mounting revolutionary tension

### Act 2: Transformation (12,500 words)

- Political upheaval affects magical barriers
- Émilie's growing awareness of her mixed-blood power
- Increasing monster manifestations
- Marriage to Charles
- Tussaud begins chronicling changes

### Act 3: Deterioration (12,500 words)

- Noble magic systems failing
- Revolutionary magic rising
- Bound entities testing limits
- Foreign Plot investigations
- Family under surveillance

### Act 4: Culmination (12,500 words)

- Arrest and imprisonment
- Red shirt conspiracy revealed
- Final ritual preparations
- Mass execution transformed
- New binding established
