# PREMISE

## Summary

In 1791, sixteen-year-old Émilie de Sainte-Amaranthe discovers that her mother's popular gambling salon serves a darker purpose. Through a hidden passage's peephole, she witnesses noble patrons performing blood rituals not to gain power, but to maintain ancient seals containing horrifying entities - creatures half-glimpsed in mirrors and dreams, bound through centuries of aristocratic sacrifice and obligation.

As revolution transforms Paris, Émilie learns that her mother's role in running the salon was carefully chosen - her mixed blood heritage makes the establishment a unique nexus where noble magic remains stable despite revolutionary upheaval. Through Marie Tussaud's careful documentation, we witness Émilie's growing understanding of how her own mixed bloodline connects to both the seals and what they contain, even as her famous beauty draws dangerous attention from both aristocratic and revolutionary powers.

By 1794, nineteen-year-old Émilie has married Charles de Sartine, becoming a central figure in Paris's shifting society. When she and her family are arrested as part of the "Foreign Plot" conspiracy, she discovers her fellow red-shirted prisoners are actually the last practitioners of containment magic, desperately maintaining seals that will break upon their deaths. In her final moments, Émilie makes a spontaneous choice, instinctively using her mixed blood to transform the power of mass execution into a new kind of seal - one that will bind the horrors permanently but end the age of magic forever.

## Main Characters

### Émilie de Sainte-Amaranthe (Protagonist)
- Ages from 16 to 19 during story
- Grows from observer to unwitting savior
- Mixed blood makes her uniquely powerful
- Beauty brings both opportunity and danger
- Discovers her power through natural instinct

### Madame de Sainte-Amaranthe (Mother)
- Maintains salon as magical nexus
- Protects daughter while teaching survival
- Negotiates between noble and revolutionary powers
- One of few who understands both magical systems
- Dies helping maintain seals until the end

### Marie Tussaud (Narrator/Chronicler)
- Creates wax works showing seal degradation
- Records increasing monster activity
- Trusted witness to both magical systems
- Chronicles final sacrifice and sealing
- Preserves truth for future generations

### Charles de Sartine (Love Interest)
- Noble who understands both worlds
- Marries Émilie despite dangers
- Helps protect family through connections
- Shares final imprisonment
- Part of red shirt conspiracy

### The Bound Ones (Primary Antagonists)
- Ancient entities sealed by noble blood
- Grow stronger as revolution weakens bonds
- Take multiple forms as they break through
- Seek to fully enter human world
- Nearly freed by mass execution

### Saint-Just (Human Antagonist)
- Senses but misinterprets magical activity
- Investigates supernatural occurrences
- Orders family's arrest
- Orchestrates red shirt execution
- Witnesses final transformation
