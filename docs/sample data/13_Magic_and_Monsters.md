## ARISTOCRATIC MAGIC

### Blood-Based Power

- Draws strength from ancestral lineages
- Requires noble blood; stronger magic in purer blood
- Some power responds reflexively, but most skills require training
- Power concentrated in individuals
- Strengthened by intermarriage between noble houses
- Weakened by "contamination" with common blood

### Ritual Characteristics

- Elaborate, time-consuming ceremonies
- Requires expensive components (rare gems, precious metals)
- Performed in private, consecrated spaces
- Highly formalized, rigid protocols
- Often incorporates Latin, Greek, or ancient French
- Relies on inherited grimoires and family tokens

### Power Sources

- Land ownership (from incorporeal monsters trapped beneath France since the rise of man)
- Divine right (connection to religious authority)
- Inherited magical artifacts
- Family curses and blessings
- Aristocratic symbols (crowns, scepters, seal rings)

---

## PLEBEIAN MAGIC

### Collective Power

- Draws strength from crowds and communities
- Power in numbers rather than bloodlines
- Amplified by shared suffering and rage
- Strengthened by collective action
- Fed by spilled blood regardless of source

### Ritual Characteristics

- Spontaneous, adaptable ceremonies
- Less like rituals and more like mob acts with occult implications
- Uses common materials (bread, wine, work tools)
- Performed in public spaces
- Informal, intuitive protocols
- Incorporates street slang and folk songs
- Passed through oral tradition and direct experience

### Power Sources

- Mob energy
- Urban spaces (streets, marketplaces, sewers)
- Popular will
- Shared grievances
- Revolutionary symbols (cockades, liberty caps, tricolor)

- Nobles slightly smarter, stronger, and faster than commoners on average

## THE ANCIENT HUNGER: SUPERNATURAL FRAMEWORK FOR REVOLUTIONARY FRANCE

### Historical Evolution of Spirit Binding

#### Pre-Human Era
- Ancient predatory spirits claimed French territories as hunting grounds
- Manifested primarily through shadow-forms not cast by physical objects
- Possessed ability to materialize and physically destroy prey
- Established supernatural hierarchies based on hunting territories

#### Early Human Settlement
- Initial human settlements served merely as prey
- Shamanic discovery of blood-binding rituals
- Development of first spirit-containment ceremonies
- Establishment of sacred sites that anchored binding spells

#### Cycles of Power

##### Gaulish Period
- Druidic continuation of shamanic practices
- Integration of Celtic magical traditions
- Enhancement of binding rituals through organized sacrifice
- Development of stone circles as binding focal points

##### Roman Period
- Absorption of local magical practices into Roman religious framework
- Transformation of blood sacrifice into formalized ritual
- Creation of underground temples to contain bound spirits
- Integration of imperial power structures with supernatural containment

##### Medieval Period
- Church adaptation of ancient binding practices
- Transformation of blood sacrifice into symbolic ritual
- Noble families become hereditary spirit-binders
- Development of aristocratic blood magic

#### Current State (1788-1794)

##### Spirit Characteristics
- Primary Form: Non-source shadows
- Materialization Capabilities: Can manifest physical forms for violent attacks
- Targeting: Specific hatred for noble bloodlines
- Intelligence: Ancient, cunning, patient
- Weakness: Vulnerability to blood-binding rituals, can be injured and killed by noble blood magic
- Nobles can fight them with magic by letting their own blood

##### Noble Decay
- Weakening of blood-binding rituals through generations
- Corruption of noble bloodlines weakening magical efficacy
- Increasing spirit activity as bindings fail
- Growing supernatural influence on revolutionary sentiment

##### Revolutionary Impact

###### Magical Consequences
- Each noble execution weakens existing bindings
- Mass executions create supernatural power surges
- Revolutionary violence feeds spirit strength
- Guillotine creates unique magical resonance

###### Spirit Strategy
- Deliberate manipulation of revolutionary fervor
- Careful targeting of knowledge-bearing nobles
- Systematic destruction of binding sites
- Preparation for full emergence

### Post-Terror Implications

#### Spirit Liberation
- Complete breaking of ancient bindings
- Enhanced power from centuries of confinement
- Systematic elimination of binding knowledge
- Establishment of new hunting territories

#### Human Impact
- Loss of protective magical knowledge
- Vulnerability of all social classes
- Destruction of protective sites and artifacts
- Emergence of new forms of supernatural predation
