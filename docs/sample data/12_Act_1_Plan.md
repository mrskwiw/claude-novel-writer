# ACT 1 PLAN: DISCOVERY
## Chapter-by-Chapter Outline

---

## CHAPTER 1: THE SALON'S SHADOW

### Opening:
Émilie helps her mother prepare salon for evening, notices subtle oddities in mirrors

### Key Elements:
- Mother's careful arrangements of specific noble guests
- First hint of supernatural: Émilie catches reflection moving wrongly
- Introduction of class dynamics through servant interactions
- Key nobles arrive, including future love interest Charles
- **Chapter climax:** Émilie discovers hidden peephole behind mirror

### Supernatural Progression:
- Subtle mirror anomalies begin
- Glimpses of wrongness in reflections
- Shadows moving independently
- Sense of hidden purpose beneath gambling

### Character Development:
- Émilie: Innocent helper observing patterns
- Mother: Careful orchestrator of hidden ritual
- Charles: Appears as potential ally with knowledge
- Servants: Fearful background presences

### Historical Context:
- 1791 Paris salon culture
- Constitutional Monarchy tensions
- Overheard revolutionary rhetoric
- Class distinctions evident in behavior

---

## CHAPTER 2: THROUGH THE GLASS

### Opening:
Émilie secretly watches salon from peephole, first ritual witness

### Key Elements:
- Nobles performing blood ritual in hidden chamber
- Glimpse of bound entity shadow testing barriers
- Mother's expert management of both ritual and public salon
- Introduction of salon record-keeper documenting noble debts and visits
- **Chapter climax:** Entity notices Émilie watching

### Supernatural Progression:
- Mirror becomes portal to hidden space
- Blood magic rituals revealed
- First true entity manifestation
- Émilie's mixed blood reacts unexpectedly
- Combat between ritualists and entity

### Character Development:
- Émilie: Transforms from observer to unwitting witness
- Mother: Revealed as powerful magical coordinator
- Charles: Shows magical training and combat ability
- Lafayette: Emerges as ritual leader and seal keeper

### Historical Context:
- Revolutionary unrest affecting magical stability
- Noble desperation in maintaining order
- Hidden depths beneath political facade
- Seeds of external magical conflict

## CHAPTER 3: MOTHER'S SECRETS

### Opening:
Confrontation between Émilie and mother about ritual discovery

### Key Elements:
- Revelation of mother's mixed blood heritage
- Explanation of noble blood magic system
- First signs of revolutionary magic in streets outside
- Charles shows understanding of Émilie's position

### Supernatural Progression:
- Noble blood magic clearly defined
- Mixed blood power mechanism explained
- Revolutionary collective magic emerges
- Weakening of traditional seals visible
- Émilie discovers unexplained magical strength

### Character Development:
- Émilie: Conscious participant moving toward understanding
- Mother: Teacher and protector with hidden fears
- Charles: Potential ally and romantic interest
- Revolutionary forces: Emerging supernatural threat

### Historical Context:
- 1791 social hierarchy under strain
- Revolutionary rhetoric gaining power
- Class conflict intersecting with magic
- Old order weakening visibly

## CHAPTER 4: WAX AND SHADOWS

### Opening:
Émilie visits prominent wax artist's studio to commission portrait

### Key Elements:
- Portrait sitting reveals supernatural sensitivity in wax
- Discussion of changing magic through revolution
- Street riot shows collective power growing
- Evidence of bound entities affecting common people
- **Chapter climax:** Émilie's mixed blood accidentally activates half-finished wax portrait

### Supernatural Progression:
- Introduction to Marie Tussaud and her awakened senses
- Wax medium captures supernatural changes
- Revolutionary magic's visible effects
- Émilie learns about plebeian magic system
- Mixed blood power activated accidentally

### Character Development:
- Émilie: Active participant learning new magic
- Tussaud: Awakened chronicler and teacher
- Revolutionary crowd: Begins showing conscious power
- The Bound Ones: Increasingly affected by revolution

### Historical Context:
- Wax museum as cultural documentation
- Street-level revolutionary energy
- Common people gaining power and awareness
- Contrast between court and street magic
