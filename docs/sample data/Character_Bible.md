## CRITICAL WORLD CONTEXT

### THE SECRET NATURE OF MAGIC
**FUNDAMENTAL RULE:** Magic is SECRET. Common people do NOT know it exists.

#### What This Means for All Characters:
- **Noble magic users:** Must hide their practices from commoners
- **Revolutionary magic users:** Don't realize they're doing magic (think it's just leadership/rhetoric)
- **Émilie:** Must guard knowledge carefully, faces danger if discovered
- **Tussaud:** Awakened to truth, now carries dangerous knowledge
- **Common people:** Attribute supernatural events to superstition, coincidence, or noble tricks
- **Revolutionary authorities:** Would execute anyone proven to practice magic (seen as aristocratic deception)

#### Impact on Character Behaviors:
- **FEAR:** All magical characters fear exposure more than physical danger
- **ISOLATION:** Cannot discuss truth with most people
- **PARANOIA:** Must constantly hide supernatural awareness
- **MISUNDERSTANDING:** Revolutionary violence has magical effects users don't comprehend
- **TRAGEDY:** Red Shirt prisoners die for maintaining seals common people don't know exist

#### Communication Rules:
- Magical knowledge shared only in extreme privacy
- Coded language when discussing supernatural in public
- Rituals disguised as gambling, political meetings, social events
- Tussaud's awakening is shocking because commoners shouldn't know
- Émilie teaching requires absolute secrecy

### REVOLUTIONARY MAGIC USERS (COLLECTIVE)
**Role:** Emerging Magical Force, Unwitting Power Wielders  
**Blood Status:** Common people  
**Nature:** Mostly unconscious practitioners, few awakened

#### Collective Characteristics
- **MOST:** Sense crowd will as mob sentiment, use it unconsciously
- **SOME:** Have sensed the subtle difference and awakened to magic
- **FEW AWAKENED:** Become powerful manipulators of crowds
- Learn through practice: sense emotion → sense its flow → manipulate to magical effect
- Don't realize they're doing magic (it feels like stirring up the crowd)
- No training, no grimoires, no formal understanding

#### Physical Manifestation
- Gather in crowds feeling strong shared emotions
- Listen to awakened manipulators who can direct crowd energy
- Channel power through collective action and rage
- Create magical effects through mass violence and shared suffering

#### Opinions & Beliefs (Awakened Ones)
- Their power comes from righteous anger and popular will
- The people's collective voice has literal force
- Noble magic is just aristocratic lies and tricks
- Revolution is breaking old mystical bonds (true, but they don't know why)
- They're natural leaders, not magical practitioners
- **DON'T KNOW:** They're doing magic at all

#### Fears (When Awakened to Magic)
- Being called out as different from the crowd
- Losing connection to popular will
- Being labeled as aristocratic or mystical (death sentence)
- Not understanding what they're doing
- That nobles might be right about something
- Revolutionary tribunal discovering their "taint"

#### Hopes (Awakened Ones)
- To lead the people to victory
- To destroy all noble power
- To perfect their ability to read and direct crowds
- To create permanent change
- To be remembered as heroes of the people
- To understand and control this feeling they have

#### What Confuses Them
- **CRITICAL CONFUSION:** Why they can sense and direct emotion better than others
- The difference between stirring a crowd and moving magical power
- Why certain words and gestures have more effect
- Strange effects around executions and mass gatherings
- Whether they're special or just good leaders
- Connection between their ability and what nobles do

#### Learning Process
1. **First Stage:** Unconsciously sense crowd emotions as "mood of the people"
2. **Second Stage:** Begin to notice patterns - when crowds are "hot" vs "cold"
3. **Third Stage:** Learn to sense the flow of emotion before action
4. **Fourth Stage:** Discover they can nudge that flow with words/gestures
5. **Awakening:** Realize they're doing something beyond normal leadership
6. **Mastery:** Learn to regulate emotion-to-action flow for magical effects

#### Powers & Abilities (Awakened)
- Sense collective emotional energy in crowds
- Read the flow of mob sentiment before it acts
- Manipulate emotional energy toward specific actions
- Amplify anger, fear, rage, grief through crowds
- Create spontaneous magical effects through mass action
- Channel execution energy (unknowingly)
- Break noble wards through collective force
- Power increases with crowd size and emotional intensity

#### Limitations
- **MUST HAVE CROWD:** Cannot work magic alone
- Need strong shared emotions (anger, grief, joy, fear)
- Power dissipates when crowd disperses
- No protection against noble magic as individuals
- No conscious control over specific effects
- Can't understand what they're actually doing
- Risk being consumed by mob they direct

#### Key Examples in Story
- Revolutionary speakers at executions
- Leaders at September Massacres
- Demagogues directing mob actions
- Those who organize spontaneous "justice"
- Street leaders during uprisings

#### Relationship to Noble Magic
- Opposite in every way:
  - Individual vs. Collective
  - Blood inheritance vs. Popular will
  - Trained ritual vs. Spontaneous emotion
  - Controlled precision vs. Wild fury
  - Centuries old vs. Newly emerging
  - Understands itself vs. Unconscious force

### THE BOUND ONES
**Role:** Primary Antagonists, Ancient Entities  
**Nature:** Pre-human predatory spirits, eternal devourers  
**Status:** Neither increase nor decrease since dawn of time

#### Physical Description & Manifestation Variations
- **PRIMARY FORM:** Non-source shadows (shadows without physical origin)
- **COMMON MANIFESTATION (like Chapter 2):** Ephemeral darkness with impossible angles, many limbs moving wrong, large and dangerous
- **SOME VARIANTS:** More solid and less ephemeral than standard
- **RARE FEW:** Can speak (most cannot)
- Can manifest temporarily for physical attacks
- Forms with impossible angles and movements that hurt human perception
- Darkness that absorbs rather than reflects light
- Too many limbs, wrong joints, movements that defy physics
- Shapes that make eyes ache and minds recoil

#### Opinions & Beliefs
- French territories are their ancient hunting grounds
- Humans are prey, nobles are specific enemies (binders)
- Binding is temporary, revolution is opportunity
- Patience is strength (millennia of waiting)
- Blood magic can be outlasted
- Freedom is inevitable
- **Pain is existence** - only feeding brings relief
- Suffering is eternal but feeding eases it

#### Fears
- Silver and noble blood magic (can disperse them temporarily)
- Permanent binding (more secure than previous seals)
- Émilie's mixed blood (unexpected threat they don't understand)
- Complete containment rather than temporary dispersal
- New forms of magic they don't comprehend
- Final transformation that creates unbreakable seal
- **Being dispersed without ability to feed** (pain without relief)

#### Hopes
- Complete breaking of ancient seals
- Elimination of binding knowledge
- Freedom to hunt and feed on human prey again
- Destruction of noble bloodlines
- Establishment of new hunting territories
- Return to pre-human supremacy
- **Constant feeding to ease eternal pain**
- Revenge against those who bound them

#### What Confuses Them
- **CRITICAL CONFUSION:** Émilie's power (mixed blood shouldn't work this way)
- Revolutionary magic's rapid emergence
- Why guillotine creates unique magical resonance
- The spontaneous transformation of execution energy
- How containment becomes permanent
- Plebeian magic's collective nature (so different from noble individual power)
- How pain-easing feeding becomes weapon against them

#### Nature & Existence
- **ETERNAL:** Cannot be killed, only dispersed temporarily
- **DEVOURERS:** Consume energy to ease constant pain
- **CANNOT STARVE:** But suffer eternally without feeding
- **PAIN-DRIVEN:** Existence is suffering unless actively feeding
- **PATIENT:** Millennia of binding has taught terrible patience

1. **Narrative Perspective:** Tussaud begins narrating first-person after meeting Émilie; before that, writes what Émilie told her about earlier events
2. **Tussaud Meeting:** During portrait sitting, where she shows understanding of plebeian magic
3. **Tussaud's Blood Status:** Complete commoner, awakened to magic through wax-work
4. **Entity Variations:** Varied manifestations, Chapter 2 entity is common but large/dangerous, some more solid, few can speak
5. **Entity Nature:** Eternal, can only be dispersed not killed
6. **Entity Motivation:** Devourers of energy, always in pain unless feeding - can't starve but suffer constantly
7. **Revolutionary Magic Users:** Most unconscious, sense crowd will, few awakened to manipulate deliberately
8. **Learning Revolutionary Magic:** Practice stages from sensing emotion to manipulating its flow
9. **Red Shirt Prisoners:** Follow historical records, all non-dead/non-fled should be there
10. **Red Shirts' Knowledge:** Know they're last, some suspect immediate seal failure, others think it takes time
11. **Émilie's Father:** Minor country noble, captain who died in wars with England
12. **Father's Blood:** Specific bloodline doesn't matter to her power
13. **Magic as Secret:** Magic is hidden from common people, they don't know it exists
