## NOTES FOR COLLABORATORS

### The Secret Nature of Magic:
Magic is HIDDEN from common people. This fundamentally affects:
- What characters can discuss openly
- Fears of discovery and exposure
- Why certain actions are necessary but secret
- How revolutionary forces misunderstand what they're channeling

### The Dual Magic Systems:
- **Noble Magic:** Individual, inherited, trained, precise
- **Plebeian Magic:** Collective, emergent, intuitive, powerful
- **Mixed Blood:** Bridge between systems (Émilie's unique ability)

### The Protagonist's Journey:
Émilie moves from:
- Innocent observer → witness → participant → defender → savior
- Protected innocence → desperate survivor → willing sacrifice

### The Horror Progression:
- Act 1: Mirror wrongness → hidden rituals
- Act 2: Public incidents → barrier failure
- Act 3: Capture → prison → seal degradation
- Act 4: Execution → transformation → permanent binding
