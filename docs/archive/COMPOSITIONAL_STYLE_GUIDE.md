### Emotion Portrayal
**Approach:** [e.g., "Physical sensations and actions rather than emotion labels"]

**Examples:**
```
✗ She was angry. (telling)
✓ Her jaw clenched. The coffee cup trembled in her grip. (showing)

✗ He felt afraid. (telling)
✓ His breath came shallow and quick. The room seemed to tilt. (showing)
```

**Method:** [e.g., "Weave into present action, triggered by sensory details or dialogue"]

## Description Style

### Character Physical Description
**Timing:** [e.g., "Dispersed through first chapter, not in single paragraph"]

**Focus:** [e.g., "Distinctive features, not complete catalog"]


### Setting Description
**Approach:** [e.g., "Filtered through POV character's perception and mood"]
**Length:** [e.g., "2-3 sentences on first appearance, details woven through action"]
**Focus:** [e.g., "Mood-enhancing details, not architectural blueprint"]

## Dialogue Style

### Dialogue Philosophy
**Approach:** [e.g., "Naturalistic but not transcript-realistic. Heightened reality."]

**Characteristics:**
- [e.g., "Subtext over direct statement"]
- [e.g., "Characters talk past each other"]
- [e.g., "Conflict in every conversation"]
- [e.g., "Interruptions, fragments, rhythm"]

### Dialogue Tags
**Preferred ratio:** [e.g., "60% action beats, 30% 'said', 10% other tags"]

**Rules:**
- [e.g., "'Said' is invisible—use it freely"]
- [e.g., "Avoid adverbs in tags ('angrily', 'sadly')"]
- [e.g., "Avoid fancy tags ('exclaimed', 'queried', 'retorted')"]
- [e.g., "Action beats reveal character"]

**Examples:**
```
✓ "I can't do this." She turned away.
✓ "I can't do this," she said.
✗ "I can't do this," she exclaimed sadly.
✗ "I can't do this," she hissed/queried/retorted.
```

### Dialogue Rhythm
**Pacing:** [e.g., "Quick back-and-forth for tension, longer speeches for emotional reveals"]
**White space:** [e.g., "Break long speeches into multiple paragraphs with action beats"]

**Example:**
```
"You don't understand." She paced to the window. "You never understood."

"That's not fair."

"Fair?" She laughed, but there was no humor in it. "You want to talk
about fair?"
```

### Subtext
**Philosophy:** [e.g., "Characters rarely say exactly what they mean"]

**Techniques:**
- [e.g., "Deflection, changing subject"]
- [e.g., "Answering question with question"]
- [e.g., "Saying one thing while actions show another"]

**Example:**
```
"Are you okay?"
"I'm fine." She wouldn't meet his eyes. Her hands twisted the napkin
into shreds.
```

## Usage Guidelines for AI Editors

**Developmental Editor:**
- Check theme integration and reinforcement
- Verify POV consistency and appropriate narrative distance
- Flag showing/telling imbalances
- Ensure scene structure follows patterns (grounding, hooks, endings)
- Validate chapter pacing matches structural position

**Copy Editor:**
- Verify dialogue tag consistency
- Check sensory hierarchy and balance
- Flag clichéd metaphors/similes
- Ensure flashback tense handling follows rules

**Line Editor:**
- Strengthen metaphors within image cluster system
- Suggest sensory details where scenes are too visual-only
- Improve subtext in dialogue
- Enhance thematic resonance through word choice

**Content Generator:**
- Use recurring imagery when generating new content
- Match narrative voice and distance
- Respect showing/telling balance
- Follow dialogue style (tags, rhythm, subtext)
- Integrate thematic elements naturally
