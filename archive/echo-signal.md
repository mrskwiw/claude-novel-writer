## Summary

Hard sci-fi thriller set in September–December 2027. Sarah Chen (32), a SETI radio astrophysicist at Green Bank Observatory, detects a signal at 2:47 AM whose encoding structure uses MIT 2019 technology — impossible given the signal originated 72 light-years away, meaning it was transmitted before the encoding existed. The signal contains three components: a star map, an unfalsifiable mathematical proof, and 23 seconds of audio in Sarah's own voice, from the future, warning of a catastrophe.

Director Harrison Morrison (NSA) arrives on Day 14, isolates the observatory from external communication, and applies pressure to James Park (Sarah's mentor) via a 2019 NDA. James sacrifices his career by releasing the NDA records publicly, removing Morrison's leverage. Sarah transmits the signal on Day 79, completing the temporal paradox. The novel ends there — whether the warning works is never shown.

## Completed Prose

- **Chapter 1 — "The Signal"** (~3,200 words): Opens in media res. Sarah detects the anomaly, runs 18 verification passes before telling anyone. Establishes her analytical voice and the observatory's eerie silence.
- **Chapter 2 — "Verification"** (~2,800 words): Encoding structure confirmed impossible. Preliminary report filed. The silence that follows the report is more ominous than any response would have been.

---

## Core Creative Decisions

- Temporal paradox is **not explained** — the point is what characters do when confronted with the unresolvable, not resolving it
- Morrison is right about everything except who has the right to decide what people can handle
- Novel ends the moment Sarah sends the signal — the reader lives with the same uncertainty as Sarah
- Narrative voice: close third, Sarah's scientific precision colors the narration; "anomalous" before "strange," "significant" before "important"
